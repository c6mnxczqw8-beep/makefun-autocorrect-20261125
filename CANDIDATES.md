# Autocorrect fail candidates (draft pass)

One line each, under 200 characters, that READS as a
hilarious autocorrect fail: the corrected text itself must
carry the disaster.

1. Casserole emoji: a condolence message whose flowers
   became a casserole emoji, footnoted too late. Sympathetic
   but edible.
2. Defiantly: "I will defiantly be there at 8" (sent by
   someone who meant 'definitely', to their boss, about a
   meeting they support). -- the classic one-word mutiny;
   the framing makes it a complete story, and it is the
   most believable fail in the set.
3. Ducking: "Sorry I'm late, the traffic is absolutely
   ducking today" -- the eternal one, still undefeated in
   group chats.
4. Regrads: "Warm regrads" signed by a manager whose whole
   email demanded attention to detail. -- professional
   self-own.
5. Mourning: "Happy birthday!! Hope your mourning is going
   great 🎉" -- one letter from disaster, party emoji
   included.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. The best autocorrect fails are one word
deep, and "defiantly" is the deepest: a loyal employee,
confirming attendance at a meeting they support, converted
by one correction into a mutineer. The asterisked
correction -- *definitely -- arriving too late, in the same
message, is the artefact every reader has personally sent.
